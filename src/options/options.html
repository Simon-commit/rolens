Where to send them

DiscordPosts to a Discord channel through a webhook, which the Discord app delivers to your phone. Create one under Server Settings, Integrations, Webhooks.
Phone notification with ntfyntfy is a free, open source notification app for Android and iOS. Install it, subscribe to the topic below, and alerts arrive as push notifications. Anyone who knows the topic can read it, so keep the generated name private.

Discord and ntfy receive only what the alert shows: the trader's username, the items with their values and the totals. Nothing is sent until you save a destination.
